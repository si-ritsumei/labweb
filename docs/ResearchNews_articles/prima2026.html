Mr. Yamauchi papers have been accepted for "The 27th International Conference on Principles and Practice of Multi-Agent Systems".

Author:Ryo Yamauchi, Yohei Murakami
Title: A SharedPlans-Based Open Collaboration Protocol for LLM Multi-Agent Systems
Abstract: LLM multi-agent systems, in which agents with assigned roles divide the work, have attracted growing attention. However, most work leaves implicit how far agents must share and align their goals, subtasks, responsibilities, capabilities, and expectations of one another before collaborating. We therefore externalize the shared plan state (SP state) defined by SharedPlans as a PlanDoc schema that LLM agents can reference and update. We further propose SharedPlans-Based Open Collaboration Protocol (SP Protocol), in which specialist agents jointly construct a plan on the PlanDoc, revise it toward unanimous agreement, declare before execution their expectations of one another and whether they can execute their own parts, and then execute their assigned actions on the established PlanDoc. On cross-domain QA from MuSiQue, we compare four conditions that differ in whether specialist agents participate in plan construction and whether the plan is shared during execution: Centralized Planner, Manager-Router, Distributed Planner, and SP Protocol. SP Protocol is the only condition that can simultaneously establish the SP-state components—agreement, owned responsibility, mutual belief, and ability belief—and attains the highest sub-action relevance (gold reasoning steps advanced by executed subactions) and the highest accuracy. Its plan quality is slightly below the Centralized Planner’s but above the Distributed Planner’s, and its no-answer rate is the lowest. The contribution of this paper is to operationalize a theoretically defined shared plan state as a PlanDoc schema and SP Protocol, and to show empirically that plans built and agreed on by specialists improve execution and accuracy over centralized and independent planning.

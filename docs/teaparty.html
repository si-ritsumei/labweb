Laboratory Life

A multicultural tea party was held at the Social Intelligence Lab!
Date: April 26th, 2023

Overview

The Social Intelligence Laboratory is a multinational laboratory with Assistant Professor Ampere from Thailand and students from China and Indonesia.
Therefore, we decided to hold a multicultural tea party by bringing sweets and tea from each country!
There were drinks and sweets from various countries, including Chinese tea, coffee-flavored candies popular in Southeast Asia, Indonesian wafers and crushed peanut snacks, and Japanese Yatsuhashi and Nure-Senbei (rice crackers).
Everyone enjoyed talking about their impressions of each country's flavors and culture while eating sweets and drinking tea they had never seen before🎵

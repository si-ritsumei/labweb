Laboratory Life

A course briefing session was held for first-year students of the Advanced Social Design course.

Overview

On June 24 and 28, we held a course briefing session for first-year students of the Advanced Social Design course, introducing research and consultation sessions with laboratory students. Even though it was late in the day, many first-year students participated on both days. During the research introduction, students played word guessing games with Professor Pituxcoosuvarn and other international students using "Langrid Chat", a multilingual chat tool used in the laboratory, and they experienced multilingual communication. Students who participated in the game said they could feel the linguistic and cultural differences! They had a lot of fun. Hopefully, this will make people more interested in cross-cultural collaboration!

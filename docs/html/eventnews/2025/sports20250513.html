Laboratory Life

Sports tournament held!
Location: OIC Arena
Date: May 13th, 2025

overview

After the seminar, we held a sports competition at the Ritsumeikan OIC Arena.
This time we challenged ourselves with basketball and 'Keidoro' (tag game of cops and robbers), running around with all our might and getting all sweaty!! The teacher, not losing to the young ones, was running with all their might and scoring a lot of shots! As expected!!
It had been a while since I seriously moved my body, and I felt the decline in my physical fitness, but on the other hand, there was no shortage of laughter, making it a great refresh!
After the exercise, we were treated to juice by the teacher and took a moment to relax together. We spent a fun time showing different sides of ourselves, unlike in the seminar. The sports tournament was my first challenge, but it was a great success!!

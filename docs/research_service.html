Services Computing

We have been working on technologies related to service computing. Our work-in-progress are listed as follows:

Quality Control for Crowdsourced Bilingual Dictionary Creation

In this project, we study and simulate crowdsourced Bilingual dictionary to optimize the quality and accuracy of crowdsourcing work. We applied several technics such as answer integration method, calculating trust values, and etc.

Decentralized Crowdsourcing Platform Based On Blockchain

Blockchain is gaining popularity and it has been applied to various field. In our lab, we are researching to apply this teachnology to crowdsorcing by decentralize crowdsourcing platform.

Service Clustering

A composite service needs several services to be functional, unless the composite service itself woule be affected. We are studying a method to find functionally close services when a particular service in the composite service is no longer available.

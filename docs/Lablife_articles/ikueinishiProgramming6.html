A programming course for 1st and 2nd grade students was held at Ikuei Nishi Junior High School.
Location: OIC
Date: July 22, 2026 (Wednesday)

Overview

A programming course for 1st and 2nd grade students was held at Ikuei Nishi Junior High School.

1st-year students used a visual programming language called Scratch to develop a "Nail Game." At first, many of them struggled with combining programming blocks and learning how to use the controls. However, through trial and error, they gradually learned how to make their games work as intended, and it was wonderful to see their smiles grow with confidence. They challenged themselves repeatedly to clear the game within the time limit, and cheers of excitement could be heard whenever they succeeded. They also seemed to enjoy the fun of creating things while discussing ideas with their friends.

2nd-year students worked on creating an "Animal Slot" using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Many students were seen experimenting with the code to see how it affected the behavior and design of their projects. They also added their favorite images to create original slot machines, and each student put their own unique spin on their creations. After completing their projects, they enjoyed spinning the slots and sharing their creations with each other, which allowed them to experience not only programming but also the joy of bringing their ideas to life.

This day was a great opportunity for each student to challenge themselves in programming and significantly enhance their thinking and problem-solving skills♪

Overview

A Subway party was held at the Social Intelligence Lab!
It was a wonderful chance for everyone to relax, unwind, and enjoy some quality time together.
Each member selected their favorite sandwich and customized it with their preferred toppings. Some opted for veggie-loaded it, while others focused on cheeses and meats, crafting their own "perfect bites."
The variety of sandwiches reflected the diverse personalities in the group, and the lively conversations—ranging from research to personal hobbies—added to the cheerful atmosphere.
The event also featured interesting presentations. Ms. Motozawa and Mr. Kitagawa shared their experiences from a recent international conference, while Mr. Sunada shared about his job-hunting. Their stories sparked inspiration and provided valuable perspectives for everyone in the lab.
It was a memorable day filled with enjoying and learning.

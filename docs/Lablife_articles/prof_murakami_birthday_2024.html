We celebrated Prof. Murakami's birthday!
Date: Dec 4th, 2024

Overview

Prof. Murakami's Birthday Party was held at the Social Intelligence Lab!
On the day of the event, laboratory members gathered to enjoy the celebration in a friendly atmosphere.
Over lunch, we enjoyed chatting about our daily research and daily life while eating Subway, a familiar food in our laboratory. The professor was smiling from start to finish, and the entire laboratory was enveloped in a warm atmosphere.
In addition, the lab members prepared a homemade cake and celebration plate! The cake looked and tasted great, and the professor seemed very impressed!
By celebrating his birthday, I feel that the bond between the members of our laboratory has deepened. We will continue to do our best so that we can continue to work together with him to advance his wonderful research.
Once again, Happy Birthday, Prof. Murakami!

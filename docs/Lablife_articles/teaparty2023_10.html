Laboratory Life

A multicultural tea party was held at the Social Intelligence Lab!
Date: October 11th, 2023

概要

The Social Intelligence Laboratory is a multinational laboratory, with Assistant Professor Ampere from Thailand and students from China and Indonesia.
For this reason, we hold multicultural tea parties bringing sweets and teas from each country!
We had more sweets than we could eat, including tea and sweets from China, Indonesian sweets, and popular Japanese sweets!
Some of the sweets were new to everyone, and they enjoyed talking about the flavors and cultures of the countries where they were from 🎵.
We are looking forward to the next tea party!

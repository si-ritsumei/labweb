Laboratory Life

We had a barbecue that also served as a social gathering!
Location: Settsu Gorge/River and Forest BBQ Land
Date and time: Wednesday, September 18, 2024

概要

We held a barbecue to deepen friendships with the newly assigned 3rd year students from the fall semester!
The venue was a rented barbecue land in Settsu Gorge. During the conversation on the bus on the way home, I could see conversations that transcended the boundaries of grade level, and I felt that the friendship between the students had deepened.
It was very refreshing to have a dinner party in an environment rich in nature, which was different from the usual digital environment, including the fact that we welcomed new members, 3rd year students.
For the food, we grilled the meat and vegetables provided at the barbecue area ourselves! It was a special experience to eat food under the blue sky with a glass of juice or alcohol in one hand!
I'm looking forward to the fall semester under the new system as everyone looks like they're having fun!
The lively fall semester is coming soon!

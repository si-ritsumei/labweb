Laboratory Life

We had a barbecue that also served as a social gathering!
Location: MIYOSHI NO SATO BARBEQUE GARDEN
Date and time: Monday, September 1, 2025

概要

We held a barbecue to deepen friendships with the newly assigned 3rd year students from the fall semester!
The event was held at the 'Miyoshi no Sato' barbecue garden located in Settsukyo.
We cooked meat and yakisoba ourselves! The ingredients prepared by our seniors were abundant, and the third-year students also ate a lot happily, allowing us to spend a lively and enjoyable time!
The weather on that day was also favorable, and there were more people playing in the river than expected, allowing us to enjoy not only the barbecue but also the beauty of nature!
I'm looking forward to the fall semester under the new system as everyone looks like they're having fun!

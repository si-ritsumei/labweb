Laboratory Life

A programming course for 1st and 2nd grade students was held at Ikuei Nishi Junior High School.
Location: OIC
Date: December 26, 2024

Overview

We recently conducted a programming course for 1st and 2nd-year students at Ikuei Nishi Junior High School.

The 1st-year students used a visual programming language called Scratch to develop a "Nail Game." While many of them were new to programming and initially struggled with the unfamiliar tasks, they showed persistence and worked through the challenges. It was great to see them naturally helping each other and staying motivated to complete their projects.
The 2nd-year students developed an "Animal Slot" game using JavaScript, CSS, and HTML. Although the content was more advanced and complex, they carefully followed instructions and worked with focus and dedication.
Through this programming workshop, it is hoped that the students discovered how fun programming can be! ^_^

Research Life

Here is an introduction to life in the Social Intelligence Lab last year.

3rd year Fall semester

・Web application development for solving foreigner problems

We will go to the Shiga International Association to research the problems faced by foreigners living in Shiga Prefecture and develop a web application.

What you will learn: GitHub, Docker, group development skills, problem solving

4th year Spring Semester

・seminar

Students will read technical books such as Introduction to Large Language Models and An introduction to the philosophy of artificial intelligence to deepen their understanding and enrich their research ideas.


・Determination of research theme and start of research

After meeting with Professor Murakami to finalize the research topic, we will begin our research!

4th year Fall semester

・research

Students will continue their research, which began in the spring semester of their junior year, and will write their thesis starting in December. The graduation thesis will be presented at the end of the program.

Other Events

The Social Intelligence Laboratory hosts a variety of events.

This year, we have prepared many events and are also looking for new event ideas

・tea party

Since there are people of many nationalities in this laboratory, we hold tea parties on an irregular basis, bringing sweets and teas from different countries.


・BBQ

Last year, we held the event at the famous Settsukyo Gorge in Ibaraki City. A small stream runs nearby, and some students even waded into the river! It was a fun and memorable time.


・Cherry blossom viewing

We held a cherry blossom viewing party at Expo '70 Commemorative Park. It resulted in further deepening the friendship of the laboratory.


・Welcome party for new students

The Social Intelligence Laboratory is a wonderful laboratory where all students, regardless of their grade, get along well!
After being assigned to a laboratory, we hold a welcome party for new students.

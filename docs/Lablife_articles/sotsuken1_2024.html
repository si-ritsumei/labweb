Third-tear students gave their final presentations for their Graduation Research 1!
Date: Jan 17th, 2025

Overview

Third-year students made their final presentations for their Graduation Research 1!
Following on from last year, we split into two teams and worked on our projects. This year's project's names were "Dialy Board" and "Shiga Chat."
Starting with fieldwork, group activities aimed at solving problems, and finally a presentation and feedback of results, these activities allowed us to reflect on what they have learned so far and experience practical, real-life group development that will lead to the future.
In terms of the development environment, this year we developed the front-end using React and built a back-end test environment using FastAPI, which allowed each group's tasks to be separated more than in previous years, making development more efficient.
On the day of the presentation, Ms. Mitsuda from the Shiga Intercultural Association for Globalization came to visit us, and we were able to show him the results of the past six months!

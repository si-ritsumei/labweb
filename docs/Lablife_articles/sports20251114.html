Laboratory Life

Joint sports tournament held with the Network Systems Laboratory (Noguchi Laboratory)!
Location: OIC Arena
Date: November 14th, 2025

overview

We held a joint sports tournament with the Network Systems Laboratory (Noguchi Lab) at the Ritsumeikan OIC Arena!
The event featured a basketball match between our labs. Both teams were determined not to lose, and the game turned into an intense and serious battle on the court. The cheers from teammates on the sidelines added even more excitement and boosted the energy of the match.
In the end, the Murakami Laboratory claimed victory, and as a reward, Professor Noguchi kindly treated us to some juice.
This joint event was a great opportunity not only to strengthen the bonds within our own lab but also to deepen connections with members from other laboratories.
